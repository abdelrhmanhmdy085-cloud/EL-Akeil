## 2025-05-14 - [Accessibility & Splash UX]
**Learning:** Splash screens without a skip option are a significant UX hurdle, especially during frequent development testing. Also, replacing non-semantic divs with buttons for interactive elements and using visible labels instead of just placeholders significantly improves accessibility and clarity.
**Action:** Always include a skip option for splash screens and prioritize semantic HTML elements (buttons, labels) over generic divs. Ensure labels are visually available and not just hidden for screen readers, as they help all users.
