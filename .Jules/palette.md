## 2025-09-03 - Driver Registration Form Accessibility
**Learning:** Adding explicit `for` and `id` associations to form controls (`<input>`, `<select>`) in registration forms allows screen reader users to understand form input expectations and enables touch/click targeting on label text for mobile users.
**Action:** Always check all `<form>` elements across registration/auth pages for explicit `<label for="...">` and `<input id="...">` pairs.
